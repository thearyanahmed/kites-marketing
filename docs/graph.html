The graph.

:graph, or gr in a note, opens a live canvas of your notes and the links between them. People are rings and tags are rounded squares.

With the mouse

ActionDoes
scrollzoom at the cursor
drag the backgroundpan
drag a nodemove it; the rest follow
hover a nodehighlight its neighbourhood
click a noteopen it
click a tagshow its notes
double-click a tagopen its tag page

With the keys

KeyDoes
h j k lpan
+ / -zoom in / out
0fit everything
n / Nstep through notes, most linked first
⏎open the selected note
thide or show tags

Tags as nodes

Every tag is a node. Child tags hang off their parent (#projectlighthouse → #projectlighthouse/api), and each note hangs off every tag it carries, so a note under two tags sits between them. Press t to hide tags and see only links.

People

People are rings. gr on a person shows who and what is within two links of them.

Labels and layout

The most linked notes are labelled from a moderate zoom, and the rest fade in as you zoom closer. Paperkites lays the graph out in the background, when it starts and shortly after you stop writing, so the page opens already settled.
