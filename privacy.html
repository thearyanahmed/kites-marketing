Privacy

website

Visits to this site are counted with Google Analytics, but only after you accept cookies. Until then, and if you decline, Google Analytics sets no cookies. Your choice is kept in your browser, and you can change it any time from . See how Google uses this data. There are no forms here, so nothing you type is collected.

purchase

When you buy Premium, payment is processed by Stripe, under its own privacy policy; your card details go to Stripe, never to us. We keep your email and order id, to deliver your licence key and to send it again if you lose it. Nothing else, and it is never sold or shared.

the app

Paperkites has no telemetry and no account. Your notes stay on your Mac. The app goes online only to check for updates, and to open the purchase page in your browser when you ask it to. Licence keys are checked offline.

contact

Questions, or want your purchase data deleted? support@paperkites.app.
